Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Welcome to Reels Tadka, an Android application crafted and provided by mSoftVision ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to transparency regarding any data accessed by this application.

Key Summary: Reels Tadka does NOT maintain, host, or store any personal user information in a remote backend or cloud database. Your authentication details remain strictly on your local device for login sessions.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

When using Reels Tadka, limited data is handled as follows:

  • Google Authentication: When you choose "Continue with Google", we use Android's Credential Manager to verify your identity. The app reads your public display name and Google email address solely to show your user profile in the app's Profile section. This information is never uploaded to our own remote server or database.
  • Local Device Storage (Preferences): Your login status, display name, and session preferences are saved exclusively on your local device via encrypted Android SharedPreferences. You can erase this data at any point by signing out or uninstalling the app.
  • Local Temporary Cache: To provide buffer-free playback of short videos, temporary media files may be saved within a dedicated local cache on your device (up to 200MB). This cache is managed locally on your phone and can be cleared at any time from your device application settings.
  • Device Identifiers: A locally generated pseudorandom ID (e.g., RT-XXXXXX) is kept on your device storage strictly to assist in user session persistence and error logging.

2. Third-Party Services

The app relies on third-party frameworks to maintain functionality:

  • Google Play Services & Google Identity: Used to facilitate secure Google Single Sign-On (SSO). Google may process identifiers in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Third-Party Content Services: Media and reels shown in the feed are delivered via public endpoints and third-party content distribution networks.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Since mSoftVision does not collect or transmit your personal data to any external central servers, we do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute any user data to advertisers or commercial third parties.

4. Security of Data

All authentication transactions are handled through standard Google Play Services OAuth 2.0 protocols. Local tokens and session preferences stay sandboxed within your Android operating system security environment.

5. User Data Deletion Policy

You have full autonomy over your session and local information:

  • Instant Local Deletion: Tapping "Sign Out" inside the app's Profile screen completely clears your cached email, name, and login credentials from your device.
  • Application Data Reset: Clearing the app cache or storage via Android Settings → Apps → Reels Tadka → Storage → Clear Data deletes all local traces immediately.
  • Support Request: If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to formally request assistance, email us at msoftvision@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Reels Tadka is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe an underage person has provided information, please reach out to us for review.
